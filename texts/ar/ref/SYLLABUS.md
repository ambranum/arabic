## The merged sequence (what a Najah/Givat-Haviva student actually walks through)

1. **Script & sound** (najah lessons 1–7): letters in functional clusters, short/long vowels,
   sukūn, sun/moon assimilation — each letter-batch immediately carried into spoken words.
   *App mapping:* the app is romanization-forward, so script stays optional; the sound
   CONTRASTS and sun/moon assimilation feed Phase 0 (Sounds).
2. **Survival topics, self and household** (speaking units 1–13): countries/capitals, family
   statuses & members, the 14 greeting↔answer pairs, days, town/village, professions (with
   role-play), host-family visit prep, food, first recipes-as-texts, travel, bus.
   *App:* Phases 1–2 — this is the Reaction + The-self material, native-sequenced.
3. **First grammar riders, taught inside topics** (najah 6–7, vocab-gram): pronouns +
   pronominal suffixes, لَمِين possession, عِنْدي / بِدّي, adjective agreement in ʿāmmiyye,
   prepositions + declension, conjunctions. *App:* Phase 2–3 grammar lessons — the books
   corroborate the app's existing 20-lesson list and add agreement + preposition-declension
   depth.
4. **The verb engine, chart by chart** (verb-forms 1–12 then weak verbs; verb-drills in the
   same order): Form 1 in four vowel classes (سِمِع، فَتَح، طَبَخ، عَزَم) → Forms 2, 3, 4, 5,
   6, 7, 8, 10 (9 omitted) → hollow (شاف، عاش، نام) → doubled (حَطّ، حَبّ) → defective (حَكى،
   خَلّى، لاقى…) → assimilated (وِصِل) → أَكَل، أَجا. Ten-person paradigms across six tenses +
   imperative/AP/PP/gerund; future auxiliary deliberately rotated (رَح / بِدّي / لازِم /
   مُمْكِن); negation drilled as transformation (ما…ش). *App:* Phases 2–5 verb track — the
   book ORDER now defines the verbTier walk; conjugation cells themselves stay engine-verified.
5. **Function-word semantics — the fluency multipliers** (verb-drills 32–41): the SEVEN
   meanings of صار, the FIVE meanings of خَلّى, حَكى vs قال. *App:* Phase 4–5 grammar lessons
   (new material the app doesn't have yet — high value).
6. **Counting & the food table** (vocab-gram): mass nouns with unit words (حَبَّة، راس، قَرْن،
   ظُمَّة), numbers with produce, the 199-item singular↔plural glossary, broken-plural
   patterns. *App:* Phase 3 grammar + Phase 6 hospitality language.
7. **Narrative + graded reading** (stories; spoken-extra): six graded stories (الجمل بدّه
   يتعلّم → فتّوش الفأرة → … → Kanafani's القنديل الصغير, one story explicitly بلهجة الجليل),
   each with a قاموس and retell/perspective-shift exercises; situational dialogues (restaurant,
   trips, the Arab house). *App:* Phase 4 (narrative) reading pools + Phase 5–6 dialogues; the
   books' retell pedagogy IS the app's 4/3/2 drill, natively confirmed.
8. **MSA bridge** (najah 8–11): imperfect conjugation, case endings, imperative, feminine
   morphology, formal readings. *App:* out of scope for the dinner-table goal (formal register);
   kept in the transcription for reference, not scheduled into lessons.

## Drill formats to rebuild in-app (Stage 3)

greeting↔answer pairs · substitution ("change the underlined pronoun, retell") · retell-from-
memory · perspective-shift retelling (وجهات النظر) · role-play prompt boxes · Q→A drills ·
ten-person conjugation fill-ins · negative transformations (ما…ش) · imperative drills (m/f/pl) ·
unit-word counting drills · dialogue completion · recipe/process retelling.
